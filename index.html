Learning that goes beyond marks and rankings

Sri Nakshatra School is a "School with a Soul" in the twenty-first century.

Every child is unique, with their own abilities, interests and talents. Our aim is to identify and nurture each child's potential and help them discover their true strengths.

We believe education is not about making children study, but about inspiring them to learn, explore, think and grow. Our focus is on moving from rote learning to meaningful learning and skill development, joining academic knowledge with real-world experience to build skills, positive mindsets, creativity and confidence.

Our ultimate goal is to empower young minds to build a safer, more responsible and harmonious community, and a better world for everyone.

What sets Sri Nakshatra apart

Our teaching is individualised to the needs of your child. Every child has a unique learning style, and we adapt our instruction to support their growth.

Language policy

English is our medium of instruction, so children grow confident in the language of higher education and opportunity.

Quality classroom

Remedial classes

Helping you climb higher

Our community is a rich mix of rural, urban and suburban neighbourhoods, and the school is a microcosm of it.

Academics

Following the CBSE curriculum, we blend choice, creativity and experiential learning to prepare students for what comes next.

Curriculum

Inspiring minds. Shaping futures.

Our curriculum opens a world of possibility, blending choice, creativity and experiential learning so students think boldly, explore deeply and shape the future. Beyond academic excellence, we nurture confident leaders, creative innovators and compassionate professionals who discover their purpose and pursue it with passion.

Rooted in respect, inclusion, discipline and empathy, our school celebrates every individual and creates a culture where every student is valued, inspired and empowered to thrive.

Making a difference

Prepared to achieve, ready to contribute.

Our students should be prepared to achieve in their chosen field of study and join the workforce ready to make a difference. It begins in the classroom, where experienced and committed teachers share both the fundamentals and the real-world applications of each subject.

The individual student is what we care about. Our programme is tailored to your child, not the other way around.

Co-scholastic

Learning that comes alive.

Studying outside the classroom brings teaching and learning to life in every subject. Students' motivation and ability to apply what they learn both rise when learning happens in a variety of contexts.

To widen their perspectives and enjoy belonging to a vibrant community, every student takes part in and contributes to as many parts of school life as they can.
